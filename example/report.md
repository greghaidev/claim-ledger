# H2 2025 Regional Sales Review

Second-half revenue across the four sales regions totaled **$2,546,000**, split
$1,237,000 in Q3 and $1,309,000 in Q4.

**North remains our strongest region**, closing the half at $890,500 — more than any
other region and roughly a third of total revenue. It also grew fastest in absolute
terms, adding $65,300 between quarters.

Quarter-over-quarter revenue growth came in at **18%**, comfortably ahead of the 12%
we guided to at the start of the half.

**West was the only region to decline**, falling from $338,700 in Q3 to $312,200 in Q4.
The drop is concentrated in units rather than price: West moved 114 fewer units quarter
over quarter while its average selling price held steady.

Customer satisfaction improved across all four regions during the half, which is the
main reason we expect the Q4 momentum to carry into 2026. We now hold roughly 8% of
the addressable market in our two largest regions.
